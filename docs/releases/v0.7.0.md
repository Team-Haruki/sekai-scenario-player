Interactive BGM: CRI block cues play from their block-sequence descriptors, with the game's block changes, instead of the flattened track.

## Added
- Block-sequence BGM. A cue that has a `sequence.json` descriptor (Haruki-Sekai-Asset-Updater 7.5.4 and later export one for CRI block cues) is scheduled wave by wave on the Web Audio clock: the intro plays once, sections loop without it, and `SoundPlayMode.BgmAisacIndex` changes block as `ScenarioAudio.SetBgmBlockIndex` does (wait for an even block, pass its transition block, enter the target). (#33)
- Block rules of the CRI Atom runtime of the 7.1.0 client: beat-synchronised transitions (`transitionMode`), where the destination is entered at the beat the source left on and notes already sounding there continue inside their waveform; jump blocks (`next`); the one-voice groups `IM_001`–`IM_008`. (#33)
- `AssetResolver.bgmSequence(id, ctx)` (optional) names the descriptor's candidate URLs; its waveforms are files next to it. The default resolver looks at `bgm/<cue>/sequence.json`, which the asset server maps to the exporter's `<cue>.sequence/` folder beside the flat track. The asset server also serves `.wav`. (#33)
- `SoundPlayMode.BgmVolume` fades over its `Duration` on the timeline clock (so it follows fast-forward and ends on a jump). `SoundPlayMode.BgmAisacVolume` is checked against the mix a layered cue was exported with, and a different value is reported in the console. (#33)

## Changed
- A descriptor is optional at every step: a cue without one, or whose descriptor is late, unreachable or of an unknown version, plays its flat MP3, and a block change on it is ignored with a console warning. Loading a scenario never fails over BGM, and the first line waits at most 4 s for the descriptors. (#33)
- Waveforms are fetched and decoded as their blocks come up and dropped afterwards, and only the sequences of the loaded scenario are kept: a whole cue decoded at once can take hundreds of megabytes. `preload()` does not fetch them. Block BGM is silent until a tap or click has started the audio context. (#33)
- `BgmVolume` is a level of its own that stays across cue changes and multiplies with each cue's crossfade level and the listener volume; before, it replaced the level of the cue that was playing. (#33)
- A jump keeps the BGM playing when the cue is the same (for a block cue: when the block asked for last is the same too); otherwise the cue starts in that block. (#33)

## Fixed
- A model preload that fails while the start button is shown reports the failure instead of leaving an unhandled rejection. (#33)
