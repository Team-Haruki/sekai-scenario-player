Listener volumes for BGM, voices and SE, so hosts can offer volume sliders.

## Added
- `setVolume(kind, value)` / `getVolume(kind)` and the `volumes` option set a listener volume (0–1) for `"bgm"`, `"voice"` or `"se"`. It is multiplied with the scenario's own levels (BGM fades and `BgmVolume` snippets, per-line voice and SE volumes), so moving a slider never fights a fade. BGM and voices change at once, SE from the next effect. The voice volume sits after the lip-sync analyser, so mouths keep moving at any volume. (#PR)
