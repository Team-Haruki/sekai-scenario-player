Voices on iOS, and less waiting: clips, backgrounds and model files load ahead.

## Fixed
- Play voices, SEs and BGM on iOS: any tap or key press, the start button's included, now starts sound, so auto mode needs no further taps. (#10)
- Start each voice with its line: the voices and SEs of the next three lines are fetched and decoded ahead. (#10)
- End a long camera pan or zoom when the next one starts: scenarios that cut a 20-second pan short no longer sit on a black screen until it would have ended. (#10)
- Keep quick background cuts on time by loading the next lines' backgrounds ahead. (#10)
- Retry a voice or SE that failed to load. (#10)

## Changed
- A costume's motions, facials and physics load alongside its model and textures, and the Live2D runtime loads with the scenario; episodes with several opening characters start sooner. (#10)

## Upgrade notes
- BGM, voices and SEs need CORS on a cross-origin asset host (voices and SEs are now fetched).
